Research

Oct 2026 Research, combinatorics, flag algebras, Python Ramsey research 2.0

New bounds for small Ramsey numbers

Two preprints [1, 2] with new bounds for small two-, three- and four-colour Ramsey numbers, released with certificates and exact checkers for every computer-assisted claim.

Theorem A (three colours). R(4, 4, 4) ≤ 198 and R(3, 4, 4) ≤ 73.

The previous upper bounds were 229 and 77.

Theorem B (two colours). R(4, 21) ≥ 260 and R(4, 24) ≥ 370.

These improve the lower bounds of 242 and 343.

Remark. The main new tool is a flag-algebra certificate whose constraints include local caps: each bounds an intersection of colour neighbourhoods by a smaller Ramsey number. Every certificate is re-checked in exact arithmetic by verifiers that share no code with the searches that found it. Both papers are preprints and not yet peer reviewed.

Oct 2025 Research, combinatorics, algorithms, Python COMP3821

Extremal Ramsey graphs

A research project for COMP3821 (Extended Algorithm Design and Analysis) on computational bounds for Ramsey numbers and the properties of extremal Ramsey graphs [3]. His contributions are below, together with a generator for 3-colour Ramsey graphs used to search for lower bounds.

Proposition C. The Extension Conjecture and the Degree Difference Conjecture are false.

Proof. A counterexample search over McKay’s database of Ramsey graphs finds counterexamples to both.□
