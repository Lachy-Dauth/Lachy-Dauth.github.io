Projects

  • Oct 2026

    Ramsey Numbers Research 2.0

    Two preprints with new bounds for small Ramsey numbers, including R(4,4,4) ≤ 198 (previously 229), R(3,4,4) ≤ 73 (previously 77), R(4,21) ≥ 260 and R(4,24) ≥ 370. The proofs use flag-algebra certificates, and every computer-assisted claim is re-checked in exact arithmetic.

    Code and certificates arXiv coming soon
  • Oct 2025

    Ramsey Numbers Research

    A COMP3821 research project on computational bounds for Ramsey numbers. I disproved the Extension and Degree Difference conjectures with a counterexample search over McKay's graph database, and wrote a 3-colour Ramsey graph generator for lower bounds.
