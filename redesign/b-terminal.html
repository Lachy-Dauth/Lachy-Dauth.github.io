  1. GitHub arXiv soon

    Two preprints with new bounds for small Ramsey numbers, including R(4,4,4) ≤ 198 (previously 229), R(3,4,4) ≤ 73 (previously 77), R(4,21) ≥ 260 and R(4,24) ≥ 370.

    The main new tool is a flag-algebra certificate with local caps on colour neighbourhoods. Every computer-assisted claim ships with certificates re-checked in exact arithmetic by independent verifiers.

  2. PDF ↗ GitHub

    COMP3821 (Extended Algorithm Design and Analysis) research project on computational bounds for Ramsey numbers and the properties of extremal Ramsey graphs.

    My contributions: computationally disproving the Extension Conjecture and the Degree Difference Conjecture by counterexample search on McKay's graph database, and implementing a 3-colour Ramsey graph generator to find lower bounds.

  3. Explore ↗

  4. Play ↗
